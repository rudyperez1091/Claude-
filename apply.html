Admissions

Apply to the academy

The academy accepts students from outside our neighborhood through the magnet programs, and students from our regular feeder pattern.

  1. Pick your program

    Look through the four tracks. Data Science & Machine Learning and AI Foundations are magnet programs open to students across Miami-Dade.

  2. Apply online

    Magnet applications are submitted through Miami-Dade's Your Choice site. Search for Southwest Miami Senior High.

    Go to yourchoicemiami.org

  3. Questions? Reach out.

    Not sure which track fits, or how the application works? Contact Vicky Lazo. Students and parents are both welcome to reach out.
