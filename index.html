Four ways into tech

Two magnet programs open to students from anywhere in Miami-Dade, plus two tracks for students in our regular feeder pattern.

  • 01

    Data Science & Machine Learning

    Magnet

    Collect, analyze and tell stories with data. Learn SQL and database design, build dashboards, and train machine learning models on real problems.

    Course map →
  • 02

    Artificial Intelligence Foundations

    Magnet

    The “Five Big Ideas” in AI: perception, reasoning, machine learning, human-AI interaction and societal impact, with hands-on coding and a focus on ethics.

    Course map →
  • 03

    Game & Simulation Programming

    Track

    Storyboard characters and worlds, program single and multi-player games, and take a playable game from prototype to release with a design team.

    Course map →
  • 04

    Web Development

    Track

    HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP. Design sites people enjoy using, connect them to databases, and learn e-commerce, SEO and analytics.

    Course map →
Beyond the classroom
