NAFTrack certification

Get hired before you graduate

NAFTrack Certification shows employers and universities that you have real-world skills. Certified students get access to NAFTrack Certified Hiring, with priority consideration and direct interviews at leading companies.

Requirements

  1. Complete all required academy coursework
  2. Complete a qualifying paid internship
  3. Meet all high school graduation requirements
  4. Log at least 100 hours of documented community service
  5. Pass two industry certification exams

Open the NAFTrack student site

Career readiness & support

  • Résumé review and one-on-one interview prep
  • Coaching and feedback after interviews

Hiring & internships

  • Access to paid high school and college internships
  • Guaranteed interviews when competing against equally qualified candidates
  • Priority hiring and faster advancement through hiring pipelines

Pay & college

  • Potential for higher starting salaries in entry-level roles
  • Connections to colleges where NAFTrack partners actively recruit
Scholarships

Money for college

Amounts and deadlines change every year, so always check the organization's website. Questions? See Mrs. Maytee Perez, CAP Advisor.

Juniors: register for the SAT and the ACT and take both before the end of junior year. Many scholarships require a score.

ScholarshipAwardWho can apply
Amazon Future EngineerDeadline usually in December Up to $40,000 + internship Seniors planning a bachelor's in computer science, software, computer, mechanical or electrical engineering, robotics, or a related field. Includes a paid summer internship offer at Amazon.
NSA Educational Scholarship Full tuition + salary Seniors majoring in computer science or computer/electrical engineering. U.S. citizen, eligible for a security clearance, SAT 1200+ or ACT 25+, preferred GPA 3.5+. Includes year-round salary and a job at NSA after graduation.
The Gates Scholarship Full cost of attendance Pell-eligible seniors with a 3.3+ GPA, from an eligible minority group, planning to attend a four-year college full time.
Coca-Cola ScholarsSchool CEEB code: 101-102 $20,000 Seniors with strong academics, leadership and commitment to their community.
Horatio Alger National Scholarship $25,000 Juniors with critical financial need, community involvement and a 2.0+ GPA who have overcome adversity. U.S. citizens.
Horatio Alger Career & Technical $2,500 Students entering a career or technical program of up to two years who are Pell-eligible and under 30.
Hispanic Heritage Youth Awards Varies Seniors recognized for achievement in categories including technology, business, entrepreneurship and community service. Awards are presented at regional ceremonies, including one in Miami.
Heisman High School Scholarship $1,000–$10,000 Seniors with a weighted GPA of 3.0+, who play at least one sport and show leadership at school and in the community.
Elks Most Valuable Student Varies Seniors. See the website for requirements.
YoungArts Up to $10,000 Artists ages 15–18 (grades 10–12) in dance, film, music, photography, theater, visual arts, voice or writing. Fee waivers available.
Army ROTC Varies Seniors ages 17–26, GPA 2.5+, SAT 1000+ or ACT 19+. Requires a service commitment after college.
Air Force ROTC Varies U.S. citizens with an unweighted GPA of 3.0+ and SAT 1240+ or ACT 26+. Must pass medical and fitness tests.
Southern Scholarship Foundation Free housing Seniors with a 3.0+ GPA and financial need. Rent-free housing near FSU, UF, FAMU, Bethune-Cookman and FGCU.
