Magnet program

Data Science & Machine Learning

For tomorrow's data professionals. You'll build the technical, reasoning and problem-solving skills needed in an increasingly data-focused world.

  • Data analytics & visualizationCollect and analyze data, then tell a clear story with visualizations and interactive dashboards.
  • Programming & machine learningCore programming, relational database design (SQL), and machine learning algorithms for real-world problems.
  • Ethics & societal impactData privacy, common cybersecurity issues and responsible data governance.

Four-year course map

  1. 9th
    • Procedural Programming
  2. 10th
    • Data Analytics and Database Design
  3. 11th
    • Machine Learning and Applications
    • AP Computer Science
  4. 12th
    • Capstone Project with Industry Partners
    • FIU Dual Enrollment COP 1000 · CGS 2060
    • AP Cybersecurity

Summer internship must be completed before the start of 12th grade.

Academy track

Game & Simulation Programming

Bring your imagination to life. This track builds the technical, creative and problem-solving skills behind real games.

  • Game design & storytellingConcept art and storyboards, character archetypes, story plots and what makes players feel immersed.
  • Hands-on programmingProgramming for single and multi-user games, including the math and physics behind them.
  • Industry-standard productionThe full development cycle, from market research and prototypes to release, working in design teams.

Leads to careers like Game Designer, Game Programmer and Game Software Developer.

How the academy works

Built on the NAF model

The Academy of Information Technology is part of NAF, a national network that connects high school students with colleges and employers. M-DCPS hosts 62 NAF academies across 30 high schools.

Structure

A small learning community that introduces you to the wide range of careers in the digital workforce, and builds personal, analytical and technical communication skills.

Curriculum

Theme-based classes that mix academics with hands-on work, so you build the thinking and problem-solving skills colleges and employers look for.

Advisory board

Industry partners advise the academy and work directly with students, including on the 12th-grade capstone project.

Work-based learning

Guest speakers, worksite tours, career fairs, job shadowing, mock interviews and internships.

Industry certifications

Earn them in class

These credentials show real-world skill and give you an edge in college admissions and the job market. Students earn one or more in their program of study.

  • IT Specialist: PythonPearson
